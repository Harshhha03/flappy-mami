Put your files in this folder with EXACTLY these names:

face.png        - your friend's face (square, cropped close to the face)
background.jpg  - background image (portrait, ~800x1200 works well)
music.mp3       - background music (loops while playing)
flap.mp3        - short sound played on each flap (optional)
hit.mp3         - short sound on game over (optional)

Missing files are fine: the game falls back to a plain face block,
a sky gradient, and silence.
